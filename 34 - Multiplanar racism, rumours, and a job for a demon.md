
Investigating what happened in the last 22 days, we learn:

- Most of **Port Krazak** exploded after an experiment, and the port is not functional at the moment and not open for trade.
    
- Elandel's mist barrier is weakening and people managed to get in without an invitation.
    
- More dungeons are opening in the **Aerinth Jungle**, it's a minefield over there and there is a lot of traffic of people much like us, seeking adventure and loot.
    
- We talk to other adventurers and some have also met **That Guy** and had similar experiences; he says a few words then dies, says nothing then dies, or leaves after a moment. When they've unmasked him, they found the exact same face: an elf they did not recognise. Presumably cloned. He seems really weak, he falls over after a hit or two, no one's had problems defeating him.
    

The plot thickens.

Cormul wants to celebrate the attack on Port Krazak because "they're the worst stingy diggers". Also rioters and terrorists, second only to the **Bluefoot**. He begins ranting about racists, those Dwarves are the scum of the earth, and the Elves are no better! I mutter to Krozzot "sometimes it's better to nod and smile, right?" and the Orc responds "Smile and wave, boys… smile and wave." Then Krozzot makes vague references to sacrificing people, and Cormul leans to me to say sometimes it's better to smile and nod.

Krozzot was doing research on the **lightning blessing**, but found nothing. He ordered some promising titles at the library, but they'll take a while to arrive. We discuss increasing our resistance to lightning, or trying to make the blessing stronger.

Sevaris mutters he needs to stock up on tobacco and I chime in saying I need bananas, which brings us back to the topic of racism somehow. Cormul buys a spellheart, a **Jolt Coil** to increase his resistance to lightning-type attacks. I do the same and struggle to get it on my armour.

Cormul and Krozzot get into an argument about breaking a curse, Cormul claims he is immortal. I'm a bit confused. We **teleport** to Port Leon where we left our ship. It's early afternoon and we do a bit of shopping before setting sail. The whirlpool marking the entrance to the Prison dungeon is still there, so we drop anchor and row there in the little boat. Cormul rambles about being chosen by the black smoke.

We end up in a dreary prison ~~and I clip through a wall by mistake~~. We find **undead and demons** locked up, and the rest of the party looks for their allies here but… we find no one. Krozzot theorises Wobble ate them, but Cormul is offended. Sevaris asks one the demons, who says the people who live here tend to leave every now and then. The fiend seems to be telling the truth. The boys are very polite with the demon, who is polite back. I am thoroughly weirded out…

We ask the **demon** what he is in for. "Well, a bit of this, a bit of that… I'm a demon. I'm scary." Cormul says racial profiling, then. The demon doesn't remember who put him here, it's been a while. The **runes** on his cell can be dispelled, others have been. He's heard other demons scream, run around, destroy stuff… and then stop. They either escaped, or were killed. Sevaris casually asks the demon about what he's good at, what he does in life. "Killing," the demon says. Cormul frowns and says we are looking at least for genociding.

Sevaris is looking to hire the demon as a **guard** for our manor. I oppose the idea and get accused of racism. The demon offers a contract, he is good at that too. Krozzot points out that's more of a devil thing, and this guy is definitely a demon. The boys argue in favour of freeing and hiring the demon. I'm getting a headache. Krozzot promises to look for a good job for him, and return.

We step into the circular room with the five cells and glowing runes. One of the cells is still empty, the ritual seems incomplete. We look for a place to leave a **sneaky teleportation circle** and select a piece of floor underneath a rug. Will that interfere with the spell, though?

"That's a definitive maybe. It could even be a possibly." - Cormul

Sevaris begins preparing the circle, and closes the door to concentrate, it will take a few hours. Krozzot is offended by my racism, apparently, and offers to give Cormul a "religious experience", as he is **a man of faith** after all. Cormul is not interested in the cuck chair.

I go to the kitchen area and begin preparing snacks, in case the friends return. Krozzot checks on Sevaris every now and then. I find an oven and set to baking a luxurious banana bread, enough for a dozen people. That's dessert. Then I use the last of the scorpion meat to make a spicy seafood stew. Is scorpion seafood? It's close enough to lobster…

Sevaris flies out of the room chewing on a slice of Archon loaf. I hand him a piece of still-warm banana bread, and he complains it's too light and fluffy, he'll never get full from this. I cry a little bit. Eventually we decide to eat dinner before dessert, and **our friends don't return**. We play card games despite the lack of motorcycles, and then return to the ship to spend the night on board.

Sevaris mentions he wants to visit **Elandel**. Krozzot grumbles he'll just speak Demonic and throw fire around, and camouflage as a balloon again. The wizard claims he won't, he is a changed man. None of us believe that.

We have a quiet night on the water and Cormul grumbles like an old man in the morning. I emerge from the galley to serve Porridge with all the toppings to everyone. The news of the many dungeons draws us to the jungle… and we kind of want to see the barrier around **Aerinth** up close, so we sail south-east. We drop anchor in a nearby bay.
