
We continue exploring the strange burrow dungeon. More bedrooms and kitchens, then we open a door and see a square room with a similarly **square pool** in which the water looks distorted. I almost spy a grin flashing in and out… There is skelebones right next to the pool. Medicine check! Is the pool a toilet and did he die of explosive diarrhea? Nat 20. Looks like he **died of fright** and then just fell over. Rest in peace, skelebones. I close the door and the boys ask me if I didn't want to keep the bones for stock. I argue I prefer to know where the bones come from, and I prefer them to be from a freshly killed creature.

I poke my head through the other door and see a small room filled with **treasure**, and a pile of Wobble, Cormul, Tony, and Sevaris counting coins. They've got this.

Sevaris is zooming around the burrow and triggers another lightning trap, but nimbly dodges all of it.

"What the hell are those black things?" - Krozzot.

"They're shitters." - Cormul.

"They're unlit braziers." - The DM.

"Wow… so unlit." - Krozzot.

Past the unlit braziers, Sevaris zooms to a door and as he opens it, it explodes and spikes shoot out at him! With the door gone we walk into a small mess hall and Wobble proceeds to eat everything on the tables. Sevaris chugs healing potions like they're going out of fashion. Krozzot's Orc superstition is tingling and he goes ~~on his own adventure~~ **down a flight of stairs**. Sevaris is trying to fly through the walls and eats another trap for his troubles.

The floor explodes into lightning and he is hit this time. Sevaris proceeds to complain to God about traps, which is rich, being one himself. Cormul steps into a room with his family, the door shuts behind them, and **poison gas** fills the room! I know nothing about this as the party is split 4-way. I finally find Sevaris just hovering in front of a door that's being banged on with a lot of Cormul screaming.

I charge into the door, pauldron first, and crash through. Now the poison gas is slowly spilling out… Sevaris gets this look in his eyes and I know he's wondering how **flammable** this gas is. Time to leg it! I see Cormul got the same read as he hurries in the other direction. As I attempt to exit the inevitable explosion radius, I hear Sevaris chuck a flame into the room and a massive bang and shockwave politely inform me that the gas was indeed quite flammable.

As I run from the insanity pyrotechnics put on by the trap fairy, I of course step on another lightning trap. Grumbling, I realise Krozzot is nowhere to be seen. I shout at Sevaris as I go through a cloud of smouldering smoke, and he casts **Message** to say in my head… "What?!"

Fairies were a mistake.

Tony dodges more ground lightning with a nat 20. I spot Wobble's leaves and Sevaris flies over to join us, then zooms away again sipping more ~~bathwater~~ **healing potions**. Cormul is looting and grumbling it's not divisible by four.

I walk into a kitchen with a large firepit. I go into the pantry and see more carcasses on stone tables; behind me Wobble goes for the animals roasting over the fire and they **explode**, catching me in the blast.

Sevaris is farming Hero points through the process of spending HP and healing potions, apparently. Cormul and his squad step on another lightning glyph, Cormul's hair is smoking and he looks a little beaten up. He is unfazed and continues to rage-loot. He is looking at a throne in a room where trophies hang from the wall, and gets some **bad vibes**. I get close and feel for bad vibes but get none.

Cormul and me argue about the throne, and he orders Wobble to smash it. As he does, a **blood-curling scream** erupts and Wobble takes damage, which is funnelled entirely to Cormul. We find Sevaris and request a healing potion, then continue on The Search for Krozzot (TM). We go down stairs and arrive in a cave where a ledge lines the wall and descends into water.

We find our Orc man and he immediately remarks Cormul looks like he needs stabbing. Krozzot is speaking in tongues as he stabs Cormul and even the DM can't understand it.

"See you in ten minutes." - Krozzot.

"… Sevaris, you have a healing potion?" Cormul, who likes to gamble but also likes to win.

Krozzot catches up to me to do the stabbing, way too happily still. No healing ensues and I grumble. We find prisons, with another skelebones in it.

We start thinking on how to close the dungeon. We haven't seen a living soul… The boys mention masks, and Sevaris darts off to get one and bring it into the ritual room. **Ghostly lizardmen** come up out of the floor ~~singing the Pillar Men theme, bass-boosted remix~~. They hum a short song, then phase back into the floor, and the mask is gone.

Cormul pisses into the spring in the cave. The water rises up, forms the shape of a **skull**, and shoots a shadowy spike at him, completely missing although Cormul didn't move an inch. He peacefully finishes his piss, and the walls of the dungeon begin to wobble… the dungeon is closing! From a piss?! We rush out and discuss, theories abound but we aren't sure. The most sensible theory is Sevaris': "triggering all the traps closed the dungeon". Cormul's theory is: "there is salt in piss, and that exorcised the ghost, also he fled out of shame for missing me with the shadow spike".

I do wonder who is right.

As we come out of the dungeon, we are hit by a **wave of dizziness** and struggle to stay on our feet. It passes quickly, but then we notice guards walking about. I greet them and they are shocked to see us. They ask if we came out of the dungeon; I say yes and Cormul says no. They ask what was in the dungeon, if we saw any of the guards who were sent in. We say no, there were only bones.

Two guards were sent in a week ago. We ask what day it is… **22 days have passed** since we went in! It felt like we were inside for an hour or two at most…

"Can I wait here for GTA 6?" - Novator.

The guards say they will report this, and I hope the missing guards can be found again. We mutter among ourselves, we're never had this kind of time bullshit happen.

We decide to go home to catch up on what we've missed, and check on **Axe**. Krozzot received a message from his cult about a ritual to send the bodies back; the only downside is that he is going to need a living demon or devil to serve as a power source for the ritual. Krozzot immediately calls Sevaris over, as he checks all the boxes.

- Wings? Check.
    
- Evil? Check.
    
- Loves fire? Check.
    
- Speaks Demonic? Check.
    

Conclusion: Sevaris is not a fairy, **he is an imp**. Krozzot asks him to stand in the circle for a bit, in exchange for knowledge on rituals. Sevaris takes payment upfront. Krozzot somehow convinces him, sets up the ritual, puts Sevaris in the middle… and the magic doesn't work. Huh. Sevaris was not a demon?! Krozzot plays fair and still talks his ears off about rituals for a while. They decide to visit the Prison dungeon again to get a demon, and store the bodies for the time being.

Wait a second… Sevaris shakes his head, looks around, sees Cormul and me are missing and glares at Krozzot. "You don't tell a SOUL about this," he says and casts **Summon Fiend**. A winged abomination not entirely unlike Sevaris appears, and he promises it payment later. Krozzot's ritual only takes a minute, so it lines up with how long the fiend stays in this realm. As the ritual progresses and each corpse disappears, the fiend screams out in pain. At the end, it crumbles and disappears as well, and Krozzot's boys have been sent back to a better (?) place.

We leave them to their rituals as Cormul and me go off to buy some alcohol and see **Axe**. He is home and greets us. Cormul explains we were in a dungeon, and time stuff happened. He doesn't usually go into dungeons.

Axe tells us he went to visit **Rom the Necromancer** to hand him some bandits he'd been clearing out, as materials. We talk about the plot in Port Leon with the brain-washing bell. Axe found out the **Bluefoots** had contracted some powerful spellcasters to dull the city so they'd have an easier time taking control… and keeping control. They knew leadership in Port Leon changed often so they meant to stay in power until they could find Cormul. The spellcasters went their own way and decided to mind control everyone instead, to recruit more people into their cult.

Cormul laughs, the original plan did work, the Bluefoots were in control until they found him! Axe has no intel about the **cult**, so we decide to ask Krozzot and investigate if we can. Port Leon seems to be back to normal, the church has been repaired and no one seems to know what really happened. We talk for a while, then bid goodbye to Axe and return home to see what our friends have been up to.

We learn Krozzot's boys have been laid to rest, and ask if he has any idea about the cult that worked for the Bluefoots. He has a couple thoughts about it, but the DM keeps it secret for now.

We still need a bathhouse with massage parlour and sauna, and an inn. Sevaris and Cormul discuss what else they want to build; a mansion for us, a bakery where I can sell treats, a courtyard with a fountain.
