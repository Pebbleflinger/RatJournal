
We are down one Mr Quain tonight so Cormul is uncommonly quiet.

Sevaris wants to turn into a monkey and journey to the West. He wants to cast **Speak with Plants** but doesn't know that spell. Krozzot brings up that the bad vibes area probably continues into the lake, but it'll be difficult to ascertain how far it goes. Something in the mainland that's scaring the fish? Now that's wild.

I bring up the possibility of magic bullshit again. Sevaris mentions a cantrip he knows, **Detect Magic**, what if he heightened it? He grumbles and brings up a stronger spell instead, **Umbral Journey**. We rub our chins looking at the map. We decide to use both spells in conjunction, the first to scan for magic, the second to travel to another location to scan again.

Sevaris begins with Detect Magic, recasting it for an hour as he flies around. The spell picks up some kind of **high-rank magic**, and points south-ish. We travel south through the Netherworld, how strange. He is guiding us to the exact spot he intended, and a **Hero Point** too. Scanning for magic again, he feels a source in the east. We decide to take the scenic route east to not miss anything.

As we near the foot of the volcano, the spell's direction become more precise. With the last of the daylight we find the source of the bad vibes. There is nothing visible, and we don't feel any different. Sevaris is positive that this unremarkable spot is the source. Krozzot suggests **sacrificing something**. There are no animals, so I suggest sacrificing Sevaris. The orc is not convinced, you wouldn't get a lot of blood from him.

Sevaris is not paying attention to our antics as he is casting **See the Unseen**. He spots blurry spheres floating around the source, but can't make out what they are. ~~What do your fairy eyes see~~? He tries **Read Aura** in combination with Detect Magic. Read Aura doesn't ping anything so… not objects, some kind of creatures.

I ask if there could be a connection with the volcano. They don't think so, and Krozzot brings up the **floating flames** we saw one night. I volunteer to touch one, I can take hits. Sevaris directs me to one of the invisible things, we are both directionally challenged and it takes some work. Eventually my hand passes through a warmer spot, kind of like when you go for a swim, and somebody pisses in the lake.

We argue about whether there could be something underground or in the air, but Sevaris shakes his head. We ~~pitch a tent~~ set up camp, I cook some food, twice as much for Wobble who wobbles happily. Night falls and floating flames appear! They spread into the woods, but one comes towards us. We start discussing why this one stuck around with us. Is it drawn to the heat of our fire, to our magic? Krozzot grumbles it might be pointing out our location for something else.

The flame appears to be waiting, expecting something. Sevaris is flying 50 feet in the air and mumbling to himself. He summons a **Fireball** and holds it up to the floating flame, as if offering a snack. The flame comes closer and Sevaris lets the spell go harmlessly. The flame moves away. I pull out a piece of bread and pspsps the flame. The flame approaches… then returns to its spawning location. Krozzot offers some non-magical fire, and we get the same result.

Sevaris flies closer to the flame, slowly and gently with no ill intentions. The flame matches his speed and returns to where it originally appeared. The fairy follows it, and I start after him. The fire drifts away a short distance; stone pillars come out of the ground, forming an **arched doorway**. The flame disappears near the doorway without going through.

Krozzot urges for caution and tries to recall reading anything on the subject. Sevaris does the same, but nothing comes to mind. I offer to go first if we are to go through. Krozzot also shakes his head, no idea. Sevaris goes to open the door, he found it, it's his. I motion to my armour and shield, to no avail.

Inside the door is the entrance hall of a castle lit up with blue light. In the distance stands a figure which we faintly recognise; he carries a **fiery sword**… "I came prepared for it this time, fucker!" Sevaris exclaims. The castle hall is lined with about 29 statues. Krozzot's eyes sparkles, so many inanimate objects to fight!

That guy is facing away from us, and doesn't seem to have noticed us. Sevaris sneaks in another Detect Magic, but he's too far to tell if the statues are magical.

"Just T-pose while you fly!" - Sevaris' advice on being quiet in plate armour.

Sevaris says he can "ensure the guy can't escape" and shows us in his spellbook… **Planar Tether**. He advises we knock out the guy, teleport to the Prison dungeon, and interrogate him. We peek into the door again… he walked to a set of stairs, his back still to us. We strategise and Sevaris casts **Shared Invisibility** on us and we enter stealthily. Sevaris snaps at me to T-pose and casts **Fly** on me. We spot the dungeon symbol as we walk in.

Sevaris is hissing at Krozzot to Recall Knowledge on the statues. Orc-man recognises them as **golems** and is sad, his plan to fight inanimate objects fails, these will animate if we set them off. They are sturdy, like 100 HP sturdy. AC? 26.

"This is TPK territory." - Novator

Sevaris flies to one of the golems… and places his little hand on it. "I think he is, you know, golem-sexual," Krozzot mumbles. The golem's eyes and joints light up, and Sevaris feels a connection to it. **He can control it**… he commands it to move a finger, and it works! He flies to another one and touches it, but gets no reaction. He sneakily flies off to try touching another one, keeping to the shadows cast by the pillars.

He goes to touch other golems, gains control of three of them, and eventually catches the guy's attention…

We decide to stop here so we can have the big fight with Mr Quain!
