
We sail to the **Prison** dungeon, out on Lake Aerinth. We discuss the **Traps** dungeon that still hasn't been cleared in over 2 weeks. Krozzot says nobody returned. Cormul starts speaking in tongues, I think he is cursed. We spot a whirlpool. If that's the entrance, it's easy to find! If not… welp.

"Feet can suck my ass." - Chosen

Sevaris is sitting in the middle of a magic circle furiously chanting. Cormul is telling me to jump into the water with my diving suit. I don't have a diving suit. I have an **aquadynamic armour**. Krozzot is our ship captain, so he should leave last ~~or sink with the ship~~. He drops the anchor at a safe distance from the whirlpool and we look for a lot of rope.

Cormul grumbles the whirlpool is a scam, that someone made it so we would all drown, and they're waiting downstream for our loot to wash up on shore. That makes perfect sense, yeah. Sevaris offers to scout the whirlpool from the sky and grabs a piece of wood. I suggest tying a rope to him so we can fish him out if he does fall in and begin to die. We talk about it for a while and come up with absolutely nothing productive, so we all hop in the small boat and row ourselves to the whirlpool. Well, I am volunteered to row us closer. Feats of strength are not exactly my specialty…

We do attach a rope to **Marlene** and uncoil it from the small boat as a lifeline. Sevaris acts as our scout and peers into the whirlpool… at the bottom of it is more water, swirling like crazy! But there is a small circle of complete blackness where there should be water.

"Sounds racist, I'm in." - Krozzot

The small boat is cramped with Big Smula the unlucky rat, Wobble the devourer of worlds, and Cormul's son Tony the skeleton sailor. I ask Cormul where **Wobble** goes when he is not summoned, he doesn't know. He didn't study for that! His dad bought his degree. Wobble probably goes to the First to hang out with G'raha Tia. Who? This, or he goes to Hell and eats all the demons.

"Blood is thicker than money." - Cormul

After getting sidetracked a lot, we drop a rope into the whirlpool. It gets sucked in and spins, and when it touches the black spot it goes taut. "A more perfect grind rail I have not seen," Sevaris says happily. Wobble wants to "go floaties" and jumps in, but Tony is still sitting on his head and screeches as they drop into the whirlpool and are flushed down until they disappear.

I proudly jump on Smula's back and we both go for a swim. Krozzot gracefully descends by following the rope, Sevaris holds on tight to his piece of wood and grinds the rope rail. We enter the dungeon and Smula falls on top of me, oof. Sevaris yells out "IT'S A KINK DUNGEON," whatever that means.

It is indeed a prison, there are bars over there… Cormul saunters over at the word "bar", he could use a drink! Not these bars… There are glowing red runes outside one of the cells, and a monster rushes to us. It growls at us trying to get out. Krozzot recognises the monster as a **demon**… that's concerning. Sevaris investigates the runes, they are a kind of seal. Not the kind that swims in the sea… the kind that keeps something in.

Sevaris speaks **Demonic** and asks "Who are you?". The demon responds something and Sevaris says "He says his name is **Jeff**!" and they all point and laugh. Jeff speaks again and his horns light on fire as he tries again to break out. We decide to leave Jeff alone for now and proceed to the next cell with runes. Jeff does not look happy and continues chewing on the bones of the skeleton next to it.

There are **shadowy tentacles** coming out of the toilets. The DM makes an uncannily good anime girl noise. Yamete kudasai… We send in the Wobble and he is grabbed by the tentacle, but it phases through him. Wobble inhales the tentacle and it dissipates. Nothing bad ever happens to people who inhale black smoke, right?

Another demon gives us the side eye. We ignore him and talk about calamari as we go south and find a third demon sealed in his cell. Cormul flips him off. The demon replies in Halfling. "You fucking what mate?!" Cormul says. "Krozzot? Un-ritual this door. I'm gonna fucking Wobble him." Apparently the demon called Cormul rude. "Chill out, **Sugon**," Sevaris says in Demonic. I ask him what he said and he translates. I ask, who is Sugon? To which of course Sevaris ecstatically replies: SUGON DEEZ NUTS.

We are worse than the demons and our DM is in despair. I award him the Sanderson meme, the greatest honour I have the power to bestow. Mr Ruka spams the door so Mr Chosen removes it, grumblingly.

We smell **delicious food** coming from the south. Wobble is dashing towards the food smell despite my warnings. We hear the typical sound of a radish diving towards innocent food and a voice shouts "What the fuck?!". There is a guy in an alcove stirring a pot. He asks us in Taldane who we are. I say we are just travellers and ask if he is cooking for the demons. He replies he is cooking for his **party members** and is not planning on sharing with us.

We go into the dining hall next door to meet the cook's friends. There are a lot of skeletons here and two decidedly un-skeleton people who look like **mages**. They say they are from Port Leon and were just exploring the dungeon, but decided to stay because "it's pretty safe here except for the locked-up demons, besides who would be stupid enough to jump into a whirlpool right?" We have a good laugh, I ask if we can share their meal.

Sevaris and Cormul are spouting a lot of nonsense so I ask the mages if they can maybe un-curse them? The lady says she can, more nonsense follows and Krozzot and me consider looking for a new party. She says she is a priestess of **Abadar** in Port Leon. Oh, Krozzot is also religiously enclined, maybe they'll get along!

Sevaris takes off his clothes to be "inspected" by the pretty lady. She says that is wholly unnecessary and he really doesn't need to show her his "small treasure". Sevaris swears it gets bigger, but turns away and bends over to pick up his robe. I turn away in a hurry and cry softly in Big Smula's fur. Krozzot and me beg the priestess to **un-curse** our companions. She starts praying and taps Sevaris' shoulder, then says there is nothing wrong with him. The fairy goes dead silent, his face becomes serious and he silently floats back.

Cormul has been insisting nothing is wrong with him, but consents to be checked just to prove he is fine. The same spell is cast on him, he frowns… and the priestess says there is a very powerful curse on him, and she cannot remove it. It's not dangerous… yet. It's a **Spirit Anchor** curse, if Cormul dies his soul is prevented from moving on and he will turn into an incorporeal **undead**. He is thrilled, he thinks that means he's immortal! He's been chosen by the black smoke.

The cook from earlier returns with bowls of stew, including a much bigger bowl for Wobble. Cormul uses his **Prescient Planner** feat to take out bottles of alcohol he totally remembered to buy in the city! Party time! I do whisper to Krozzot, I'm not losing my mind, right? This is a curse? He agrees and thinks on it. I ask if you can even bring back to life someone who turned into an undead. Krozzot mutters you could bind a soul to an object and then says out loud "It is possible to make him move again after he was turned into a ghost." Oh, like bring him back to adventure with us again? The Orc nods happily. Phew, that's relieving!

We ask the other party how they will get food, if they are staying in the dungeon. They say they will soon return to Port Leon to resupply, they have their ship right here in a pouch. Huh?! We ask Severis what this means but he is all aloof. Cormul makes Tony cry. I didn't know Tony still had tear ~~ducks~~ ducts. Cormul asks if his curse can be **inherited** by his children. No, it is bound to him and it will take a very powerful cleric to remove or… transfer it.

Well… that's scary. Cormul teases me for not knowing how curses work. I'm a hunter, not a mage. He says it's pretty obvious I'm not good at spell…ing. Spells. How rude. We enquire about how to clear the dungeon, so we don't do it by accident, not that we ever would. We're told about **cages** upstairs, of which one is empty. That's funny… we mention the people we've found in other dungeons, men in black armour who looked like a copy of one another. The men in the cages look the same?! I want to take a look.

We head to the room we were told about; it is circular and a pattern on the floor connects five cages, four of them containing a man in black armour perfectly identical to the **Ruffians** we have fought in the past. Light blue energy flows from a source near the door to the four filled cages, but it is absent from the empty cage.

Krozzot says hello and does an awkward wave. The men in the cages seem frozen and do not respond. Wobble gnaws on one of the cages. Krozzot inspects the runes on the floor, then calls Sevaris over for assistance. He uncovers the men in the cages are used to **power up** something else, but does not share this information with us. What could go wrong…

I investigate the cages themselves, they seem easy enough to open. The guys inside are standing still as statues "without any idle animations". Hmmm. Whatever these words in my head mean.

We decide to explore the rest of the dungeon and find another cell with sealing runes and a demon trapped inside. I say hello and he replies politely in Taldane?! Cormul immediately strikes up a conversation and begins to flirt. The demon mentions guards outside… well, we have figured out dungeons are **places taken from elsewhere**. The demon politely asks to be let out, of course he does. Sevaris goes to piss in a corner and Cormul says he doesn't know how to disable the runes.

Krozzot ran off to investigate a statue; it is in fact a **golem**, currently inactive.

"I think I saw my future body!" - Cormul

"You're gonna kill yourself to get the mech?" - the DM

Krozzot theorises if the power from the cage room goes out, the seals might be deactivated. Cormul wants to test the golem, but Krozzot warns him there is a saying in the cult and it goes like this: "You pull it, you tank it."

Cormul apologises to the demon about not being able to free him. I whisper to him, you sure it's a good idea to free a demon? His being nice could be **deception**! Cormul agrees enthusiastically, if he is so good at deception, we could hire him! Words fail me…

Krozzot and Cormul argue about unions, and the Halfling storms off. Some clickety-clackety sounds come towards us and a little group of **skeletons** shuffle in our direction. They go into empty cells, one skelly per cell, and don't respond to our greetings. Cormul asks the other party what all of this was about; apparently the skeletons do this after a meal. They don't actually eat, but they pretend to, by sitting in the dining hall. Then they return to their cells. I muse about how sad it must be to be undead. Krozzot says we can ask Cormul and pulls out his sword before advancing on the Halfling. They squabble some more about creating undead.

The other adventurers are playing cards. I ask whether we should report the dungeon to be closed so that they wouldn't be disturbed. Sevaris mutters we can't lie, there will be consequences. He… he's thinking about **consequences**?! He's really been un-cursed! He is acting… *gasp* REASONABLE! Cormul argues he is just being depressed.

Sevaris casts Cozy Cabin and goes in by himself, shutting the door. Sure… we'll spend the night here and decide what to do in the morning.

We level up to 7!
